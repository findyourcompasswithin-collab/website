My Story

From placing people
to guiding them

For 8 years I worked in recruitment, matching people to roles, understanding what companies actually look for, and sitting across from hundreds of candidates at different stages of their careers. I was good at it. But what I noticed, again and again, was that the problem wasn't skills. It was direction.

People came to me confused, underselling themselves, in careers that drained them or heading toward paths chosen for the wrong reasons. I could place them. But I couldn't always fix what was underneath. That's what eventually led me to coaching.

I trained as a Certified Life Coach through the School of Life Academy, combining that with my recruitment background to create something genuinely different: a practice that helps people find the career and life that actually fits them, using frameworks from coaching, design thinking, and real-world hiring intelligence.

I'm based in South Africa and work online with clients nationally and internationally. My approach is warm but honest, structured but flexible, and always rooted in the belief that the right direction is findable, for everyone.

My Approach

How I coach

Human connection first

Coaching is not a transaction. Before frameworks, before tools, before any plan, there is a person. I create the kind of space where people feel genuinely heard, often for the first time. Everything else follows from that.

Research before commitment

I don't believe in one giant leap. I believe in small, deliberate steps: research, prototype, reflect, adjust. Before any major decision, we gather information. That approach reduces fear and increases the quality of every choice.

Nature as a grounding tool

The best insights often come when we step away from screens and into the natural world. I incorporate nature-based reflection practices: walk-and-talk sessions, outdoor journaling prompts, environment as a mirror, for clients who are open to it.

Questions

Things people often ask

Coaching is forward-focused and action-oriented. We work from where you are now toward where you want to be. Therapy typically explores the past to understand present behaviour. Coaching doesn't diagnose or treat mental health conditions. It helps well-functioning people move forward with clarity and intention.

All sessions are conducted online via Zoom, which means I work with clients across South Africa and internationally. This makes scheduling flexible and removes any geographic barrier. Walk-and-talk sessions can be arranged for clients in the Western Cape.

Most clients report clarity and a shift in perspective after the first session. Deeper transformation: career decisions, life redesign, sustained momentum, typically emerges over 3 to 6 sessions. The pace depends on your openness, the complexity of your situation, and the work you put in between sessions.

That's exactly the right place to start. Not knowing what you want is not a problem, it's the starting point. Most of my clients arrive uncertain and leave with a clear direction. The not-knowing is what the work is for.
