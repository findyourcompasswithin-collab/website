I See You

You are doing
everything.

You are showing up at work. You are showing up for your children. You are managing the home, the schedules, the mental load that nobody else seems to notice. You are doing all of it, and somewhere in the middle of all of it, you lost the thread of who you actually are.

Maybe you cannot quite explain it. You are not falling apart in any visible way. But something is missing. A sense of self. A sense of direction. The quiet certainty that this life you are living is actually yours.

I know that feeling. Not from a textbook. From the inside. And I built this space because I believe that what you are carrying deserves to be seen, not just managed.

This is not about fixing you. You are not broken. This is about helping you find your way back to yourself, at a pace that fits your real life, with tools that actually work when you only have an hour to yourself on a Tuesday evening.

This space is for you if
✦ You are a working woman, a mother, or both, and you feel like you have lost yourself somewhere along the way.
✦ You are navigating a life phase that feels overwhelming and you are not sure who to talk to about it.
✦ You are at a crossroads in your career and the usual advice feels hollow or out of touch with where you actually are.
✦ You want to stop running on empty and start making choices that actually reflect who you are and what you want.
✦ You are ready to do the inner work, and you want someone steady beside you while you do.

This space welcomes everyone navigating a crossroads. The frameworks here are human ones. They work because people work.

The Bigger Picture

What you do for yourself,
you do for your children too.

When a mother is overwhelmed, her children feel it. Not because she is failing, but because children are perceptive. They adapt. They shrink themselves to keep the peace. They learn to manage her emotions instead of their own. That is not a reflection of her love. It is a reflection of how much she needs someone to hold the space for her, the way she holds it for everyone else.

Coming back to yourself is not a selfish act. It is one of the most loving things you can do for the people around you.

My Background

From placing people
to guiding them

For 8 years I worked in recruitment, matching people to roles, understanding what companies actually look for, and sitting across from hundreds of candidates at different stages of their careers. I was good at it. But what I noticed, again and again, was that the problem was rarely skills. It was direction. It was identity. It was knowing who you are when someone asks.

People came to me confused, underselling themselves, in careers that drained them or heading toward paths chosen for the wrong reasons. I could place them. But I could not fix what was underneath. That is what eventually led me to coaching.

I trained as a Certified Life Coach through the School of Life Academy, combining that with my recruitment background to create something genuinely different: a practice built on real-world hiring intelligence, life design thinking, and the kind of honest, grounded conversation that most people never get to have.

I am based in South Africa and work online with clients nationally and internationally. My approach is warm but honest, structured but flexible, and always rooted in the belief that the right direction is findable. For everyone.

My Approach

How I hold the space

You first, always

Before frameworks, before tools, before any plan, there is a person. I create the kind of space where you feel genuinely heard, often for the first time. I do not rush to solutions. I sit with you in the question first. Everything else follows from that.

Small steps, real change

I do not believe in one giant leap. I believe in small, deliberate steps: reflect, research, test, adjust. Before any major decision, we slow down and gather clarity. That approach reduces fear and increases the quality of every choice you make.

Nature as a grounding tool

The best insights often come when we step away from screens and into the natural world. I incorporate nature-based reflection practices, including walk-and-talk sessions and outdoor journaling prompts, for clients who are open to it.

Questions

Things people often ask

Coaching is forward-focused and action-oriented. We work from where you are now toward where you want to be. Therapy typically explores the past to understand present behaviour. Coaching doesn't diagnose or treat mental health conditions. It helps well-functioning people move forward with clarity and intention.

All sessions are conducted online via Google Meet, which means I work with clients across South Africa and internationally. This makes scheduling flexible and removes any geographic barrier. Walk-and-talk sessions can be arranged for clients in the Western Cape.

Most clients report clarity and a shift in perspective after the first session. Deeper transformation: career decisions, life redesign, sustained momentum, typically emerges over 3 to 6 sessions. The pace depends on your openness, the complexity of your situation, and the work you put in between sessions.

That's exactly the right place to start. Not knowing what you want is not a problem, it's the starting point. Most of my clients arrive uncertain and leave with a clear direction. The not-knowing is what the work is for.
