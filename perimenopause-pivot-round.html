The Perimenopause Pivot: A Guided Round

Six weeks. A small circle of women. The shift nobody warned you about, navigated together.

You're awake at 3am again.

Not because anything is wrong, exactly. You just can't switch off. Your body feels like it belongs to someone else. You snapped at someone you love this week over something small, and you're still carrying the guilt of it. Your moods arrive like weather you can't forecast. And somewhere underneath it all is a quiet, unsettling question: is this just how it is now?

Nobody sat you down and told you this was coming. Not really. You've pieced it together from half-conversations and late-night searches, while holding everything else together as if nothing has changed.

Here's what I want you to know: you are not losing it, and you are not doing it wrong. You're in a season your body was always going to move through. What you haven't had is a map, a steady rhythm, and people walking beside you who actually understand.

That's what this is.

What the round is

A workbook you'll actually finish, because you won't be doing it alone

Over six weeks, a small group of women move through The Perimenopause Pivot workbook together, one section at a time, with me guiding each step. You get the structure of the workbook, the steadiness of a weekly rhythm, and the relief of realising you are genuinely not the only one.

This is the difference between a workbook that waits on your bedside table and one you carry all the way through, because every week there's a call, a conversation, and a circle of women expecting you.

How the six weeks run

Simple, steady, and built around you

  • One 60-minute live call each week, with me, on Google Meet. A little teaching, a guided walk through that week's section, and open space to talk.
  • A private group space between calls, so support doesn't stop when the call ends.
  • The full Perimenopause Pivot workbook, included with your seat.
  • A steady weekly rhythm, so you move forward instead of stalling.
  • Replays of every call, so a hard week never means falling behind.

The shift we're working toward

From white-knuckling it alone, to steady and in good company

Not "fixed," because you were never broken. Just clearer about what's happening, kinder to yourself in it, and no longer carrying it on your own.

Is this for you?

Honest about who it fits

This is for you if

  • You're somewhere in the perimenopause shift, whether it just started or you've been in it a while.
  • You're tired of piecing it together alone and want company that gets it.
  • You'll show up for six weeks and do the gentle work between calls.

This isn't

  • Medical care. If you need answers about symptoms, medication, or treatment, that's a conversation for your doctor.
  • A replacement for that support. The circle sits alongside it.
  • A quick fix. It's six weeks of steady, honest work in good company.
